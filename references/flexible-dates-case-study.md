# Flexible Dates — Real-World Case Study

## Mallorca, July 2026, ZRH→PMI

| Week | CHF p.P. | vs. Mid-July |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Jul 1-8 | CHF 55 | -56 CHF (HALF PRICE!) |
| Jul 8-15 | CHF 84 | -27 CHF |
| Jul 15-22 | CHF 111 | Base |
| Jul 22-29 | CHF 112 | +1 CHF |

**Key insight:** Shifting from July 15 to July 1 saved CHF 56 per person — nearly 50% off. A 2-week shift cuts the flight price in half.

## Rule

**Always ask the user about date flexibility before running flight searches.** Even a niche shift of 7 days can dramatically reduce prices. Search at least 4-5 adjacent weeks and present a comparison table with 🥇🥈🥉 rankings.
